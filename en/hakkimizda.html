İnan Kardeşler 1974

Uzungöl İnanlar Hotel & Restaurant

In 1974, the İnan family's initiative brought Uzungöl's tourism potential to light, setting an example for the entire local community. Continuing the same vision from past to present, İnan Kardeşler keeps working with a commitment to quality service for the peace and happiness of our valued guests.
