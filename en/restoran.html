İnanlar Restaurant

İnanlar Restaurant

Along with an indoor restaurant for 160 people, an outdoor restaurant for 150 people, a meeting room and a tea garden for 100 people, you can enjoy trout prepared in different ways, daily specials and à la carte dishes, kuymak, muhlama and the famous İnan Kardeşler rice pudding. In the evenings, İnan Kardeşler offers an atmosphere where you can dance the horon and enjoy live Black Sea music. We welcome you in all four seasons to a setting that turns your holiday into happy memories.
