WHAT CAN YOU DO IN UZUNGÖL?

As elsewhere in the Black Sea region, local settlements in Uzungöl have moved onto the hills. Charming wooden facilities can be found around the lake and its surroundings.

A clear handful of water held within the dazzling green landscape of the Black Sea… The ever-lengthening reflections of two minarets, the enduring symbol of the region… Gentle clouds drifting over tired mountain peaks, and a landscape that, thankfully, has not been overwhelmed by concrete…

Uzungöl: close at hand yet out of reach, beautiful yet modest, welcoming yet reserved…

It feels as though you have parted a green curtain to gaze at a hidden jewel. Admiration, excitement and a fear of being “caught” mingle together, as if looking too long might make it vanish or close itself away. Formed when rocks falling from steep slopes blocked the Haldizen stream, the lake is the result of thousands of years of movement. It is as though a vast highland had shaken the stones from its skirts and kept a handful of water for itself.

Every moment matters: heavy rain may suddenly fall, white mist may descend and the still waters may disappear from view. Once your eyes have adjusted to this indescribable scene in Uzungöl, the fresh oxygen of the Black Sea begins to work. An unfamiliar rhythm settles in your chest: cool, refreshing and steady breaths. You are ready for a walk around the lake!

WHAT TO EAT AND DRINK IN UZUNGÖL?

Trout

Trout raised in the highland waters holds a special place and is tasted by virtually every visitor to Uzungöl. It is a favourite main course at the table. Its flavour comes from being raised in the cold waters of the Haldizan stream. The trout are raised entirely in natural surroundings in Uzungöl, and cooking them in the region’s special butter adds further flavour.

Cornbread

Ingredients: Cornmeal, hot water, salt. Method: Put the cornmeal in a deep bowl and gradually knead in hot water without allowing lumps to form. Add a little salt while kneading. Meanwhile, heat the pleki, a traditional baking vessel. Place the prepared dough in the heated vessel and cover it with a metal sheet. Spread embers over the sheet and bake. Alternatively, cook it on a pilita.

Sautéed Green Beans

Ingredients: Green beans, garlic, onion, butter, parsley, salt. Method: Remove the strings from the beans and wash them thoroughly. Cut them in half, boil, drain and set aside to cool, then squeeze out the water. Peel and finely chop the onion. Melt butter in a pan, add the onion and green beans and sauté. Add finely chopped garlic, parsley and salt, then cook for another 1–2 minutes.

White Bean Stew

Ingredients: Dried white beans, onion, diced meat, butter, suet, mint. Method: Soak the beans overnight. Fry the onion, suet and meat in butter in a pan. Add the beans and water, and cook. Finish with mint.

Pickled Green Beans

Ingredients: Green beans, water, salt, garlic. Method: Sort and wash the fresh green beans, remove their strings and wash again. Fill a pan with boiling water and leave the beans in it until their colour turns yellowish, then remove them. Spread them on a large tray to cool. Once thoroughly cool, put them in an earthenware jar with water, salt and garlic, and cover with vine leaves. Place a weight on top to keep the ingredients submerged.

Muhlama

Ingredients: Cheese, flour, butter, a little water. Method: Finely chop the cheese and mix it with flour and a little water. Heat butter on the stove. Pour the prepared mixture into the hot butter and cook until the cheese melts. In some villages, muhlama is also made with onion and eggs.

Black Cabbage Stew

Ingredients: Black cabbage, borlotti beans, vegetable oil, cornmeal, suet, salt. Method: Soak the beans overnight. Cut the cabbage into pieces that are not too fine and boil. Boil the beans and combine with the cooked cabbage. Finely chop the onion and fry in vegetable oil and suet until lightly coloured. Add the cabbage and beans. Season with salt and pepper and simmer over low heat. Once cooked, add a bowl of cornmeal, cook further and remove from the heat. Meat or kavurma may be added if available.

Black Cabbage Rolls with Meat

Ingredients: Black cabbage, diced meat, onion, rice, vegetable oil, parsley, garlic, mint, black pepper, salt. Method: Remove the cabbage stalks, wash the leaves and boil them in a pan of water. Meanwhile, prepare the filling. Chop the meat very finely and mix with finely chopped onion, rice, garlic, parsley, mint, salt, pepper and vegetable oil. Place the mixture inside the boiled cabbage leaves and roll them to the thickness of a finger. Arrange the rolls in a pan, add enough water to cover and cook over low heat.
