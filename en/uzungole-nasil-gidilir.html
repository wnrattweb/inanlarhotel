HOW TO REACH UZUNGÖL

To reach Uzungöl, you first need to travel to Trabzon. Visitors coming from Rize or Artvin can turn onto the Uzungöl road at Of.

Our charming town of Uzungöl is 99 km from Trabzon. Çaykara Tur minibuses based in the central Çömlekçi neighbourhood of Trabzon run to Uzungöl throughout the day, on average every 20 minutes. Some companies also operate minibuses from the Meydan area. If you do not have a private vehicle, you will need to choose one of these services. If you plan to drive to Uzungöl, a beautiful day awaits you.

Following the Trabzon–Rize coastal road through the districts of Yomra, Arsin, Araklı and Sürmene, accompanied by beautiful natural scenery, brings you to Of in Trabzon. Take the road branching to the right at the entrance to Of to join the Çaykara road.

The distance between the centre of Of and Çaykara is 55 km. Along this route you pass through colourful, beautiful tea gardens. You can visit the tea factories lining the road and even buy tea. After reaching Çaykara along the western side of the Solaklı Valley, you cross to its eastern slope.

On the eastern side of the valley, clear, ice-cold streams and the magnificent scenery of the Solaklı Valley leave a lasting impression as you reach Uzungöl after a 20-minute journey from Çaykara.

The rest is up to you… Enjoy a wonderful trout feast or discover the beautiful dishes of our distinctive local cuisine. After your meal, make the most of every moment in Uzungöl, a tourism paradise.

Map

By Road: There are return coach services between Trabzon and almost every province in Turkey.

By Sea: Summer ferry services connect Trabzon with Istanbul and İzmir. Various travel agencies also organise year-round ship and bus services to CIS countries.

By Air: Trabzon Airport, which is open to international traffic, provides air connections to many Turkish provinces, as well as return services to Germany, the Netherlands and neighbouring countries.
